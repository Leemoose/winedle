How to play

Guess the grape variety. Every guess is scored against the answer across nine attributes.

Country and region mean the grape’s classic home — the reference expression, not everywhere it is planted. Chardonnay grows on six continents; its answer is Burgundy, France.

Depth is colour intensity judged within the wine’s own colour category, the way WSET does it. Nebbiolo is pale for a red; Riesling is pale for a white.

Arrows on the structural tiles point toward the answer: ↑ means the answer is higher than your guess.
